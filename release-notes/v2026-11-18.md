## New Features

- 🗂️ **Kanban layout for the note list** — Switch any note list to a Kanban board, grouped by a property of your choice. The board takes over the workspace, and opening a card slides the editor in from the right. Drag cards between columns to update the note.
- 📌 **Collapse and hide Kanban columns** — Collapse a column into a narrow strip that still accepts dropped cards, or hide it and bring it back from the end of the board. Your choices are saved with the layout.
- 👆 **Click outside to close the editor panel** — In Kanban, clicking empty board space, a column background or the header closes the open note, just like the close button.

## Improvements

- 🌗 **Dark macOS app icon** — The app icon switches to its dark variant in system dark mode again, in the Dock and Finder, even when Tolaria is closed.
- 🎛️ **Tidier note list header** — Layout, group-by and sort controls are uniform icon buttons in the header, and the expand-sidebar control is back.
- 🌙 **Clearer dark-mode Kanban** — Cards stand out better from the board in dark mode.
- 🌍 **Translated Kanban labels** — Screen-reader labels for the board, columns and cards follow your app language.

## Stability and Fixes

- Checking for and downloading updates no longer gives up when the server is slow to respond.
- The AI panel no longer jumps around on its own while a reply streams in.
- Notes converted to a sheet keep their rows and formulas after reopening.
- Opening a very large HTML file no longer freezes the app, including after a restart.
- Notes with many embedded whiteboards load faster and scroll smoothly, since offscreen whiteboards load only when needed.
- Deleting CLAUDE.md no longer shows a "Claude compatibility shim" warning when AGENTS.md is present.
- On Windows, the app no longer crashes when you shut down, restart or log off.
- Linux .deb and .rpm installs now explain how to update through your system package manager instead of failing in the app.
- Fixed an error that could appear while editing a table.
- Fixed a crash with "Maximum update depth exceeded" in the note list.
